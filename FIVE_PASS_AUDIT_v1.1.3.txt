RESULT: PASS — 5/5 gates passed for source/package release.

Scope of this patch
- Restore the original official SF2 workbook used by v1.1.0/v1.1.1.
- Restore the four attendance states required by the SF2 legend:
  blank = Present; full-cell X = Absent; upper half shaded = Late Comer/Tardy;
  lower half shaded = Cutting Classes.
- Keep the click cycle Present -> Absent -> Late/Tardy -> Cutting Classes -> Present.
- Compact generated SF2 print/preview copies by hiding unused learner rows according
  to the current roster (normally populated from SF1), without deleting or rewriting
  official static labels, totals, summary rows, signatures, formatting, or print setup.
