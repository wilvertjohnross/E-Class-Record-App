E-Class Record App with GS and SF9 — v1.1.4

Release: SF2 Diagonal Compliance Marks / Signature Centering
Base: v1.1.3

Scope
- Keeps the official SF2 workbook unchanged on disk.
- Uses the official cell's existing dashed bottom-left-to-top-right diagonal as the attendance divider.
- Absent: adds one solid top-left-to-bottom-right diagonal, creating an X with the official dashed diagonal.
- Late Comer/Tardy: shades the upper triangular half of the diagonally divided cell.
- Cutting Classes: shades the lower triangular half of the diagonally divided cell.
- Generated SF2 places Adviser and School Head/Principal names centered across the existing AD:AI signature lines.
- Existing roster-sized SF2 print compaction remains unchanged.

Compatibility
- v1.1.0-v1.1.3 SF2 data remains readable; legacy T continues to normalize to Late/Tardy.
- v1.0.27-era data remains compatible.
- Attendance calculations, SF2-to-SF9 synchronization, grading/transmutation, ECR, GS, SF9, persistence, IPC, and updater security are unchanged.
