# KLAS v1.5.7 release notes

- Added the uploaded subject icon pack to the app. Original PNG artwork is preserved; consistent light holders, sizing, spacing, shadows and text labels improve presentation and theme contrast.
- Adviser Summary of Final Grades opens a subject dashboard: Regular has eight cards in two rows of four; Special Science has ten in two rows of five. Small windows wrap into two columns for readable controls.
- MAPEH is one dashboard card, opening Music and Arts and PE and Health separately without changing their existing aggregation.
- Each subject opens a three-term grade table with computed final grades. Manual entry accepts whole grades 60–100 or blanks. Invalid values restore the previous saved value. Changes feed SF9 through the existing grade-resolution path.
- Uploading subject grades is available from the dashboard and subject pages. Valid imported subjects switch to manual/uploaded values. Previously detected Class Record values can be selected again without deleting saved manual grades.
- Automatic Class Record detection remains available as a grade-source option. Existing matches and safeguards remain.
- Special Science uses Enhanced Math, Enhanced Science and Creative Technologies, with level I–IV selectable. The two distinct elective slots use the existing eight-subject catalog and show the matching elective artwork.
- No changes to grading formulas, transmutation or rounding. Teacher and SF1 learner identities remain separate.

Package is unsigned until the included local signing workflow runs. Not installed, committed or pushed.
