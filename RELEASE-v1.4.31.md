# KLAS v1.4.31

WW and PT default to five and three items respectively; their item names are read-only and add/remove controls are removed. HPS remains editable with existing validation. Existing nonstandard item counts and scores are preserved. Category weights and examination controls are unchanged.

Validation: overview and nested routes, keyboard disclosure controls, WW/PT defaults and locked controls, HPS persistence, source/payload parity and signing script syntax. Signed installation not tested.
